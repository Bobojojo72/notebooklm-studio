BP555 control map

Every control on the Better Pack 555e and what it does. Keypad shown is the automatic model.

1 2 3
  1. 1

    Main Power Switch

    I on, O off. Beeps when ready. Switching off clears Automatic Mode.

  2. 2

    Heater Control Dial

    OFF, five dots, MAX. Halfway is enough in a heated building.

  3. 3

    Water Level Slider

    Right side of the water tank. Towards the machine for wetter tape.

Heater underside is hot. Handle by the grip tabs or wait 30 minutes.

  1. 4

    + Plus

    Adds half an inch (13 mm) to the next length. Press it before the length.

  2. 5

    A Automatic Mode Automatic

    Press for on: beep and red light. Press again for off, which clears the stored lengths.

  3. 6

    - Minus

    Takes half an inch (13 mm) off the next length. Press it before the length.

  4. 7

    2x Double

    Doubles the next length. 2x24 gives 48 in.

  5. 8

    Length buttons 6 to 45

    Press one and that length feeds out straight away. Long models 12 to 90 in, metric models 15 to 145 cm.

  6. 9

    Random Hold to feed

    Tape feeds for as long as you hold it. For odd lengths.

  7. 10

    REPEAT START

    Feeds the last length again. In Automatic Mode, starts the stored program.

  8. 11

    REPEAT 2 ENTER Automatic

    Feeds the second-to-last length. In Automatic Mode, stores the length you just dispensed.

Source: Better Packages 555e User Manual, Rev 3.1 Supplied by Cotswold Packaging Group. Jo Lavene, 07930 404517
