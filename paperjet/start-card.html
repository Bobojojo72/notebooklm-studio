PaperJet start to finish

Keep this by the machine. Sprick PaperJet paper cushioning system.

Switch on

  1. Plug in Foot switch and hand control panel into the machine, then power lead to the wall.
  2. Emergency stop out Pull the red button out if it is pressed in.
  3. Rocker switch on The display lights up.

Load paper switch off first

  1. Roll: push into the roll seat, swing the roll lock in front, knob clicks in.
    ComPackt: stack on its shelf, cut and remove the bands.
  2. Open the paper guide cover Catches both sides, lift until the support pin holds it.
  3. Roll only: push the dancer arm to release the brake, paper under the arm with a loose loop.
  4. Between the deflector rollers
  5. Triangle round the paper guide Push in until the gear wheel grips.
  6. Close up Release the support pin, close the cover and catches.
  7. Switch on, hold manual feed until a closed pad appears at the outlet.

No gloves, tie hair back. Two people for a 27 kg roll or 24 kg stack.

Make pads

  1. Turn the knob to choose a mode, 1 to 5. Its yellow light comes on.
  2. Knob: push to pick a value, turn to change it, push to confirm.
  3. Press the foot switch or hand button.
1 Length
Same length every press. 30 to 1015 cm.
2 Removal
Next pad made as you take one. Esc before the last pad.
3 Manual
Runs while held, cuts on release. Hold the knob 1 s to save the length.
4 Series
Up to 3 lengths and counts, one press. Esc ends it.
5 Preset
Keys 1, 2, 3 on the right of the panel each store a length and count.

Quick fixes

Dark display
Plugged in? E-stop out? Hood closed? Switched on?
Light flashes
Paper chute flap open. Close it.
Paper tears
Lock the roll. Blow the roll brake clean.
Jam
Off and unplug. See the maintenance card.

End of shift

Rocker switch off. Weekly: unplug and clear paper scraps from under the service panels.

Emergency stop stops everything. Keep it in reach. Not for clearing messages.
