BP555 start to finish

Keep this by the machine. Better Pack 555e gummed tape dispenser.

  1. Fill the water bottleRoom-temperature tap water. Cap on tight. Bottle on its shelf, cap around the post in the tank.
  2. Fit the brush and tankShortest bristles towards the machine, lying flat. Tank locked onto its shelf.
  3. Set the Water Level SliderTowards the machine for wetter tape, away for drier.
  4. Load the tapeSquare-cut end, under the upper tape plate and feed wheel, up to the blade. Close the lid.
  5. Main Power Switch to IWait for the beep. The machine is ready.
  6. Set the Heater Control DialOFF to MAX. Halfway is enough in a heated building.
  7. Press a lengthTape feeds out wet and cut, straight away.122436
  8. Adjust first, then the lengthHalf an inch more, less, or double.+24 = 24.5 in   2x24 = 48 in
  9. Repeat or feed by handREPEAT START same length again. Hold Random for an odd length.
  10. Check and sealTape evenly wet and oily to the touch. Half on one flap, fold, smooth from the middle out and down the ends.
  11. End of shift: Main Power Switch to OThis also clears Automatic Mode. Let the heater cool 30 minutes before touching it.

Automatic Mode

Next piece feeds out as soon as you tear one off. Up to three lengths, on a loop. Automatic models

  1. Press A. Beep, red light on.
  2. Press a length, e.g. 24. Apply it.
  3. Press REPEAT 2 ENTER to store it.
  4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 for up to three lengths.
  5. Press REPEAT START to run.

H pattern: store 24 6 6. Turn off with A. That clears the program.

Quick fixes

No tape
Plugged in? Switch on I? Lid fully closed and square?
Dry tape
Slider towards the machine, refill the bottle, wash the brush.
Jams
Upper tape plate in place, tape path clean and dry.
Wrong length
Every piece off? Feed wheel needs adjusting: call maintenance.

Hot heater. Hold it only by the grip tabs. Sharp blade: cut-resistant gloves.
