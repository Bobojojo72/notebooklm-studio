Heat and water

Heater Control Dial: OFF, five dots, MAX. Halfway is enough indoors.

Water Level Slider: towards the machine for wetter tape.

Automatic Mode Automatic models

  1. Press A: beep, red light on.
  2. Press a length and apply the tape, then REPEAT 2 ENTER to store it. Up to three lengths.
  3. Press REPEAT START. Each piece feeds out when you tear off the last, on a loop.

H pattern: store 24 6 6. Press A or switch off to stop. Both clear the program.
