PaperJet care and jams

Checks, cleaning and clearing a jam with the special tool (spanner) supplied.

Switch off and unplug before any cleaning or jam clearing. Keep the plug where nobody can put it back in.

Clear a paper jam

  1. Switch off and unplug.
  2. Open the service hood.
  3. Undo both hand screws, tip them aside towards the pivot shaft.
  4. Open the embossing shaft cover, pull out the paper.
  5. Turn the knife shaft with the special tool until every scrap is out.
  6. Close the embossing shaft cover and the service hood.
  7. Plug in, switch on.
  8. Remove paper message: check, then push the knob.
  9. Paper removed: push the knob. The knife resets itself.

Every shift

  • Plugs fully home
  • Hood and paper guide cover stop the machine when opened
  • Emergency stop works
  • No paper build-up, dirt or torn paper
  • No loose screws on the service panels

Weekly clean

  1. Switch off and unplug.
  2. Open the service panels underneath.
  3. Blow or wipe out paper dust and scraps. Build-up is a fire risk.
  4. Close the panels. Never run with them open.
  5. Check cables for loose plugs, rubbing or damage.
Use
Compressed air, lint-free cloths, isopropanol.
Never
Abrasive cleaners or lots of water.

Faults

Dark display
Power, E-stop, hood. Overheated: off 5 to 10 min.
Stops mid-run
Too hot. Off 10 min, clean thoroughly.
Paper tears
Lock the roll, blow the roll brake clean.
Pads open
Tighten the star screws on the rotary shaft.
Light flashes
Close the paper chute flap.

Service on the display: book a Sprick-trained service, about every 500 rolls or 300 ComPackt stacks. Knife shaft guard is checked every six months.
