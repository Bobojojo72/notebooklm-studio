BP555 cleaning card

Glue builds up in the brush, tank and blade. Clean on this schedule to keep the tape wet and the seal strong.

Power off first. Main Power Switch to O and unplug.

Heater is hot. Let it cool 30 minutes. Hold only the grip tabs.

Blade is very sharp. Wear cut-resistant gloves near it.

WeeklyOperator

Brush

  1. Lift out the tank and take out the brush.
  2. Work the bristles in warm water with mild soap until the glue is gone. Overnight soak is best.
  3. Rinse under running warm water.
  4. Refit: shortest bristles towards the machine, lying flat.

Water tank

  1. Empty it and wash in warm soapy water.
  2. Scrub the bottom and corners with a low-scratch brush.
  3. Rinse well under running warm water.
  4. Slide it back until it locks onto the shelf.

Water bottle

  1. Fill a quarter to half full with warm soapy water.
  2. Cap on, shake hard, drain.
  3. Rinse with warm water and let it dry fully.

Hard water? Use filtered water.

Dust

  1. Remove built-up dust with a dry brush or a blower.

MonthlyMaintenance person, power disconnected

Blade

  1. Gloves on. Scrape the glue off the blade with a scraper.
  2. Do not scrape the cutting edge. It damages the blade.
  3. Light coat of light machine oil on the back of the blade.

Felt pads

  1. Add a few drops of light machine oil. No more.

AnnuallyMaintenance person, power disconnected

Inside the machine

  1. Clear all bits of tape, dust and debris with a dry brush or blower.

Heater and covers

  1. Clean the glue off the bottom of the heater and the pressure plates.
  2. Wipe all covers with a damp rag.

As neededAfter a jam

Upper tape plate and tape path

  1. Slide the plate out towards the back, holding its bent top edge.
  2. Check the tape path is clean and dry.
  3. Slide it back under the guide bar near the blade, seated around the feed wheel. Without it the tape jams.

Ready to run again?

  • Brush flat, tank locked on its shelf
  • Bottle full, cap around the post
  • Upper tape plate seated, lid closed
  • Main Power Switch to I, wait for the beep
  • Set the Heater Control Dial
  • Test piece: evenly wet, feels oily
